Discovering properties which, for their own nature, can facilitate preparation and control of
quantum systems is of strategic importance for quantum science and technologies. The proposal fits into this context, in the spirit of Ilya Prigogine's idea that the complexity around us can be simplified when we deepen the description of the system.
It will delve into the exploitation of indistinguishability, a fundamental property of composite
quantum systems made of identical subsystems (particles of the same species, such as photons, atoms, electrons, nuclei). Extending previous results, we will investigate identical particle interferometry as an inherent resource for robust quantum state engineering and entanglement generation in complex networks, mitigating the environmental noise in quantum information tasks. The analysis, grounded on preliminary findings for two particles, will be generalized to many-particle systems to study scalability and robustness of the processes. The project will carry on the theoretical analysis and develop feasible proof-of-principle schemes to be realized in suited experimental platforms, such as quantum photonics and cavity-QED.
