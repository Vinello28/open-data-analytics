In 1975 Milan, working class Patrizia Reggiani meets Maurizio Gucci at a party. The two quickly fall in love and marry, against the wishes of Maurizio's father, Rodolfo. Without his father's approval, Maurizio embraces blue collar life with the Reggiani's. But the ever-hungry Patrizia urges Maurizio to ingratiate himself with his uncle and family patriarch, Aldo.Exasperated by his eccentric and weak-minded son Paolo, Aldo teaches Maurizio the ropes of the family business.
