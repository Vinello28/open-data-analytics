In The Journey Andrea Bocelli explores Italy on horseback along the ancient trail known as the Via Francigena. Peace. Beauty. Nature. Music. Culture. There are waypoints in each episode of the pilgrimage to rediscover life's greatest joy. Merging world-class musical performances with intimate conversations set in the awe-inspiring scenery of the Italian countryside, The Journey is an exploration of the moments that define us, the songs that inspire us, and the relationships that connect us to what matters most in life. The Journey is more than a travel show, it is an invitation to recapture the wonder of life. As the worlds begins to reemerge from a year of seclusion, Andrea explores the lessons we have learned: to find hope in uncertainty, to look for the beauty in our surroundings, to be present in each moment, and to treasure each individual life. In every episode, Andrea Bocelli journeys on horseback along Italy's Via Francigena, an ancient road traveled by pilgrims for centuries.
