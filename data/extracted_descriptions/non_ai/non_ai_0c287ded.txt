“I, Elvis Riboldi” recounts the adventures and misadventures of an energetic, scatterbrained, impulsive, reckless and slightly destructive boy called Elvis. Together with his inseparable friends Boris and Emma – and that spoiled brat called Dumont too – he lives all kinds of adventures in which we combine antic, absurd humour with fantasy and action.
