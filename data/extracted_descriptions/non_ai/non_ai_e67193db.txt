This proposal is to build a flexible laboratory capable of simulating realistic mixed environments encompassing IT, cloud, OT and IoT technologies, for the purpose of testing cybersecurity risk assessment methodologies and models and verify their effectiveness in detecting relevant risks against specifically simulated scenarios and their ability to capture data driven indicators apt to evaluate security controls mandated by best practices and emerging regulations. By leveraging a mix of virtualisation technologies and physical equipment, and including common software typically part of real-world organisations both open source and commercial, this proposal aims to achieve both the ability for the lab to scale to the size required for realistic evaluations as well as to include in the defined scenarios key elements often overlooked because of constraints of generalisation. Such laboratory will be instrumental particularly in measuring methodologies based on a data-driven approach and will enable to collect evidence on how such approach can steer the results towards removing the biases that affect traditional risk assessment practices based on estimations made by people involved in management and operations of processes and systems.
