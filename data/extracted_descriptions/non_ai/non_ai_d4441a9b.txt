Venice, 1947. In a beautiful but decaying palazzo, HERCULE POIROT explains how when he was a boy, he placed a glass of water on his nightstand and prayed that God would prove his existence by knocking it over. When nothing happened, Poirot concluded that God and the supernatural did not exist. And now, Poirot looks up at the nine SUSPECTS before him, dismissive of the idea that ghosts may have committed two murders in the house. Suddenly, Poirot glimpses ALICIA DRAKE (20) among the suspects and watches in horror as Alicia floats up into the air while glowing. As she shrieks in pain, Poirot suddenly awakens from the nightmare with a gasp. Poirot’s home in Venice is besieged by various PETITIONERS all eager for the famous – but now retired – detective to help solve matters important to them.
