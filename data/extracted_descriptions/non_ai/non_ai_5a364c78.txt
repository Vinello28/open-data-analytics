At the ripe age of 6, Charlie only has one thing on her mind: saving the planet! And to do that, she can count on her new best friend, Compostman, a big environmentally friendly monster who is a total misfit in this world but so awesomely perfect for imagining a different one! And their incredible ideas for changing the world often sweep Charlies’s big brother Zack up into the tornado, when all he really wants to do, is live his own tween life to the hilt. So this tireless trio, their parents and friends are carried along in the Zero-Waste quests and maxi-mischief to becoming a super adventure full of comedy, and we learn something new about our wild and everyday lives!
