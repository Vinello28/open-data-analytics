Season 3 is effectively a story of identity – the messy life lessons of growth – who you want to be and who you actually are – and the struggles and stumbles along the way. It’s also the story of an emperor and empress in 18th century Russia so the stakes are very, very high. For Peter and Catherine, they are trying to figure out who they are as husband and wife, father and mother and their connection to the throne OR lack thereof. By the end of the series, one will flourish, becoming one of the most formidable leaders in Russia’s history - and the other will die. This season represents the most extreme journey of growth and change we’ve seen for Catherine thus far in the series – as a leader, a wife, a person. Having decided not to kill her husband at the end of S2, Catherine is wading into the business of being a leader, the trials and tribulations of which test the limits of her ideology, delving into the murky business of political compromise, backroom deals and secret handshakes.
