Superkitties is an 11-minute animated comedy-adventure preschool series, featuring aspirational characters in high-octane super heroic action, which is funny... because they're cats.In each episode, the adorable kitties are called to fix a problem in the city, sending them on a heroic adventure full of twists, turns, laughs and challenges. Overcoming impossible odds, they restore order and peace, making the world a little kinder, and a little more in need of a lint roller.
