In Season One, our brilliant anti-heroine Livia Drusilla emerged, transforming from wide-eyed innocent to ruthless political operator. She hides a fateful secret–she has sworn to restore the Republic after the death of her husband –but to do that, her husband has to survive, and so does she. At the end of Season One, Livia orchestrated the murder of Marcellus to protect her own sons, Drusus and Tiberius. She got away with it, just, but suspicion still swirls around her, her narrow escape has come at a high price, and Livia has debts to pay. Season Two picks up again in 19BC, when Gaius and Livia return to Rome after three years away in the East. After the killing of Marcellus, Livia has struggled to repair the marriage on which all her power and ambition depends, but it will face threats both old and new.
