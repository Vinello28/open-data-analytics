Les Triples chronicles the comic escapades of three loveable children— each of whom has his or her own unique way of looking at the world. Yet despite their very different points of view, together they make a successful and fun team as they explore each new surprise or challenge in their young lives. Young viewers will easily recognize different aspects of themselves in each of these energetic characters, identifying with them as the trio learns to navigate the same daily situations that the viewer may face. But it is the Triplets’ unique and humorous approach when confronted by something new that may very well inspire children everywhere to look at new experiences in a whole different light. Throughout their lighthearted adventures, the Triplets will meet a variety of memorable characters drawn from real life— characters who will show them even more ways of looking at the world around them.
