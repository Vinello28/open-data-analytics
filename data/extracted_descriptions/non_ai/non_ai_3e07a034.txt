Bond 25, the latest instalment in the James Bond film franchise, follows on from the events of Spectre. With Blofeld safely behind bars Bond has a chance at living a normal life for once. But this peace is short-lived when his past soon comes back to haunt him. An old friend appeals to Bond for help on a simple assignment. Against his better judgement, Bond agrees, and the mission turns out to be far more treacherous than expected. Bond is drawn into a web of intrigue towards a mysterious figure
