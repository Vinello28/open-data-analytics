When a struggling ex-cop is forced to return to his home state of Florida to find a Philly mobster’s runaway girlfriend, what should be a quick gig becomes a spiraling journey into buried family secrets, sunken pirate treasure, and an increasingly futile attempt to do the right thing in a place where so much is wrong. Florida Man, from creator Don Todd, is a wild odyssey into a sunny place for shady people in the spirit of BODY HEAT and Elmore Leonard’s OUT OF SIGHT.
