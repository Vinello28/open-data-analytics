Act-first-ask-questions-later CIA agent JJ (Dave Bautista), now the father-figure to young teenager Sophie (Chloe Coleman from the first film), finds his most harrowing assignment yet—he has been roped into being a chaperone for her unruly school choir, who has been invited to tour Italy. After an extremist group kidnaps Chloe’s best friend/the son of JJ’s boss to ransom for codes needed to arm the weapons for their next attack, JJ not only has to rescue the young boy and stop the terrorists from destroying the Vatican, but he must also contend with Chloe growing up, having interests outside of learning to be a cool spy like him, and even asking for romantic advice (awkward!).With thrilling action sequences, humor, heart, and exotic locations, this movie shows that while change may be uncomfortable initially, it ultimately allows us to become closer to the people we love.
