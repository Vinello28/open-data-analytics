Abstract We will develop a state-of-the-art transcriptome resource focused on colorectal carcinoma, glioblastoma, pancreatic adenocarcinoma and melanoma. The resource will be composed by a comprehensive collection of mRNAs, including those encoding for cancer-specific antigens, and long-non-coding RNAs. Furthermore, we will comprehensively characterize RNA interactions with chromatin, the translation machinery, and other macromolecular complexes, to provide functional insights when analyzing putative function of RNA in cancers.
