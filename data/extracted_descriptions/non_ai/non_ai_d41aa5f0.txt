SIX UNDERGROUND takes director Michael Bay back to his roots – the smart, fast, character-driven action he first brought to the world with BAD BOYS and the ROCK. From the hot writers of Deadpool, SIX UNDERGROUND is a fun, edgy, tough action-thriller that begins a daring new adventure franchise. Led by star Ryan Reynolds, the film follows six characters that have joined together to try and do good in the world, albeit in an unconventional way.
