Based on the Hans Christian Andersen book and bringing the Disney 1989 animated classic to live-action, THE LITTLE MERMAID tells the tale of a curious mermaid princess who yearns to know the human world and, as such, makes a dangerous deal with an evil sea witch in order to become human for three days and complete the impossible task of convincing a human prince to help forge peace between their worlds by falling in love—without the ability to use her voice.
