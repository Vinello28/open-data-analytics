To help save the harvest FRANZI (34) returns to her home in South Tyrol, after her father JOSEF (62) got injured in an accident. To settle the future of the family vineyard she’s got to endure her fathers stubbornness, fend off the other winemakers of the region and manage all the work of the harvest. Then there’s MARCO (39), and Franzi rather fancys him. But as it turns out, his family’s got a connection to Josef, which links the South Tyrolean independence movement with a family secret.
