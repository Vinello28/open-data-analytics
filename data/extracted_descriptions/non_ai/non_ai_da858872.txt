Terry Jacobs is a recently paroled felon forced to go to undercover for an Italian detective working in Roma. Terry is ordered to get near Griff, the second in command in a mafia family working on the largest Fentanyl deal in European history. Terry wants no part of this. He was thrown in prison trying to find $100,000 euros in a hurry to get a heart surgery for his young niece back in Belgium. But Terry is stuck there with the threat of being thrown back in prison for a longer sentence. On his first day undercover, the Cosa Nostra is alerted to the six-man undercover unit going after them and Terry is the last man standing, who must take them down on his own now. One-by-one the undercover officers are picked off as Terry and Griff get ready for the huge drug deal. Griff puts Terry through a series of tests to prove his loyalty and make sure he’s not an undercover agent. He enjoys making Terry squirm.
