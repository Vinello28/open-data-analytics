It is 1917 Europe was a continent ravaged by war. The ugliness and horror of the fetid trenches spilled from village to village, unleashing the ravenous, indiscriminate and deadly influenza pandemic. The unfettered industrial revolution dumped soot and waste into the outskirts of the cities. Nietzsche’s reverberation, “God is dead!” fomented a rash of uncompromising and brutal ideologies to dominate and coerce the masses.
