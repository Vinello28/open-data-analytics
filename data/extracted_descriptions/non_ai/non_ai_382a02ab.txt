PUPSTRUCTIONfollows the adventures of the world's furriest construction company, Pupstruction, and their newest captain: an innovative young Corgi named Phinny. Phinny is the leader of the team and, while he might be the smallest pup on the crew, he proves that you don't need big paws to be a great builder when you have a big heart and big dreams. The series is about the power of family and teamwork and the importance of community as the pups pour mud, sweat, and a little drool into keeping their beloved town in pawsome shape!
